About

Jordan H. Hsieh

I am a researcher working across robotics and aerospace systems, with a focus on physics-informed learning and reliable control.

At Penn's GRASP Lab, I investigated quadrotor flight in confined spaces. Earlier work at NCKU and Tensor Tech took me from plasma modeling and autonomous energy systems to satellite attitude control. I earned an M.S.E. in Aerospace Engineering at the University of Michigan.
