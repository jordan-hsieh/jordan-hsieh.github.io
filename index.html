Reliable autonomy in a changing world.

I study how robots can learn from physical systems and remain reliable when their environment and dynamics change.

My work spans confined quadrotor flight, plasma propulsion, satellite control, and distributed energy systems.

Featured Project

University of Pennsylvania · Robotics

Quadrotor flight through narrow tubes

I combined reduced-order aerodynamic models with learned residual dynamics to predict confinement effects. The approach learned from less than 30 seconds of flight data and reduced tracking error by up to 76% on hardware relative to a nominal controller.

First-author manuscript in preparation for IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters.
